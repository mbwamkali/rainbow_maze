Rainbow Maze

Find your way from the doorway in the upper left to the star in the bottom right, changing color as you go.

Difficulty
Advanced settings

How to play

  • You start white, and white can only walk on white.
  • Checkered doorways join two colors. Anyone can step onto one, and you take on the color of whatever square you step onto next, in any direction.
  • Start at the doorway in the upper left…
  • …and reach the star. You'll need to become every color on the way.
A color walks on itself, on white, and on any color it contains.

Rainbow Maze

Start at the doorway in the upper left and find your way to the star in the bottom right. You start out white, and white can only walk on white. Striped doorways join the colors: anyone can step onto one, and whatever square you step onto next, you take on its color — in any direction. A color can walk on itself, on white, and on any color it contains: magenta (red + blue) walks on red and blue too, but not green.
